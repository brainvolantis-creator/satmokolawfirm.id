01 · Prenuptial Agreements

Clarity on assets before the wedding day

We draft asset-separation agreements that are legally valid in Indonesia, tailored to each party's business and wealth situation — not a generic template pulled off the internet.

  • Joint consultation with both prospective spouses
  • Drafting & registration in line with Marriage Law
  • Adjustments for cross-border companies or assets
02 · Inheritance Planning

Wills built to prevent disputes, not trigger them

Most inheritance disputes happen not because the law is complicated, but because the plan was never written down clearly. We help draft wills and inheritance structures aligned with the Civil Code or the Compilation of Islamic Law, based on your family's situation.

  • Asset and heir mapping
  • Will drafting & supporting documentation
  • Support during the actual distribution of the estate
03 · Divorce & Marital Assets

Resolution that prioritizes outcomes, not drama

We handle divorce and marital-asset division with a mediation-first approach, litigation only when truly necessary — always protecting our client's privacy and dignity.

  • Mediation & negotiation over marital asset division
  • Representation before Religious / District Courts
  • Protection of custody and children's interests
04 · Personal Asset Protection

Separating business risk from family wealth

For business owners and directors, we help structure personal asset ownership so it isn't automatically exposed to the legal or financial risks of the company being run.

  • Review of personal asset ownership structures
  • Separation of personal assets from business entities
  • Multi-generational succession planning
