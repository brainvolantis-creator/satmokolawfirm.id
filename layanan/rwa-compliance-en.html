The Legal Bridge Between Real-World Assets, Regulation, and Blockchain Technology

The Problem: Tokenization Without Legal Is a Time Bomb

Many RWA platforms in Indonesia are stuck because of 3 things:

  1. Asset Legality: How to legally prove that a token represents a property or securities asset?
  2. Regulatory Compliance: Conflicts between UU P2SK, POJK 27, UU ITE, and UU PDP.
  3. Investor Trust: Banks & institutions require a Legal Opinion from a registered Law Firm.

Our Solution: The 3-Layer "Bridge"

We don't just give legal advice. We build the bridge down to the technical layer — with cross-border legal compliance bridging across Indonesia, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates (Dubai & Abu Dhabi), backed by the compliance systems built by Arsiva. For European Union matters, we connect you with our additional legal partner network.

Regulatory Update (July 2026): UU PFII was officially enacted on 21 July 2026, opening a new pathway alongside the existing OJK Sandbox (POJK 3/2024) — each with different tax implications and structuring flexibility. Before structuring begins, we recommend a regulatory pathway analysis with Arsiva.

Layer 1: Legal Structuring

Designing SPV structures, tokenization agreements, trustee agreements. Ensuring tokens are recognized as proof of ownership under the Indonesian Civil Code.

Layer 2: Regulatory Compliance

Legal Opinion & Due Diligence based on UU P2SK, POJK 27/2024, POJK 3/2024. Ready for OJK & bank audits.

Layer 3: Techno-Legal Integration

Integration with the on-premise tokenization platform from Arsiva — our engineering team, purpose-built for our RWA compliance needs. Smart Contracts + KYC/AML + Audit Trail admissible in court.
